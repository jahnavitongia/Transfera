# School of Computing product pilot

Presentation: 19 November 2026, 01:00 IST. Audience: university academic and admissions staff. One laptop, no paid inference service. Pilot programme: B.Tech. Computer Science and Engineering (Core), with a second fictional route from an undergraduate IT programme. Both routes enter semester 3. Fictional admissions records, clearly identified on screen and on documents. This is a university-themed student project.

## Research translated into product decisions

- The [university website](https://mituniversity.ac.in/) groups information under Academics, Admissions and constituent schools. Use “MIT School of Computing”, “programme”, “semester”, “Department of Computer Science & Engineering” and “Loni Kalbhor, Pune” where they identify an actual programme or office. Keep student navigation by task: Request a transfer, Cancel admission, Track a request.
- The [Computing programme page](https://mituniversity.ac.in/academics/faculty/faculty-of-engineering/school-of-computing/programs-offered/) lists B.Tech. CSE Core and specialisations. Its AY 2026–27 refund table is explicitly for **new admissions**. Timing depends on notice received versus the formally notified last admission date. Record receipt time in Asia/Kolkata; refer applicable cases to Accounts. Returning students and transfers cannot be given an automatic refund quote from that table.
- The [published CSE curriculum](https://naac.mituniversity.ac.in/NAAC/1_1_1/18_SOC/BTCE_2022-23_CORE.pdf) contains course codes, credits, teaching hours, prerequisites and outcomes. For example, its Java Programming Lab lists 2 credits and four practical hours per week. Use this structure to write original fictional first-year modules; do not move that published lab into semester 1 or imply that the older PDF is the 2026 syllabus.
- Section 19 of the [2016 Academic Ordinances](https://mituniversity.ac.in/assets_web/pdf/about/Academic_Ordinances_2016.pdf) describes same-level external study during enrolment, source eligibility, course documentation, departmental scrutiny, Dean approval, a 50% cap and exclusion of transferred credits from SGPA/CGPA. Incoming admissions and programme switches have a different context. Specify them as pilot decisions and leave institution adoption to academic staff.
- [W3C form guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/forms/multi-page/) supports logical groups and visible progress. Use Profile → Academic evidence → Review and submit. Preserve entries on Back, put errors by their field, and indicate who acts next. For comparison, put both sets of outcomes on the same screen. Use persistent labels, keyboard focus and status text. Test at 200% zoom and the [WCAG normal-text contrast target of 4.5:1](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html).
- Keep the supplied MIT-ADT wordmark in its original proportions on white. Draw the primary colour from the purple crest, use a fine gold divider, charcoal text and white forms. Use the device's sans serif for forms and tables, 16 px body, 14–16 px labels and a 28–32 px page title. One primary action per form. Show the pilot designation near the identity. Avoid chat bubbles and decorative AI badges.

## Proposed academic rules for the pilot

Policy ID SOC-PILOT-2026-01. Store the policy and curriculum versions on each new application. Older BCA decisions keep their original version and awarded credits. All numerical choices below are pilot settings, subject to faculty approval.

| Decision | Evidence and pilot rule | Outcome or exception |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intake | Prior undergraduate computing study, entry semester 3, with transcript covering semesters 1–2. Capture sending institute, programme, level, academic year and grading scale. | Incomplete evidence goes back for clarification. Unknown grading scale needs departmental review before award. |
| Curriculum | Filter by school, programme, target curriculum version and completed semesters. Compare theory to theory and labs to labs. | A missing catalog entry stays unassessed. Programme switches retain unmatched destination requirements. |
| Eligibility | Passing grade ≥4/10 on the declared sample scale; source credits ≥ destination credits. Require a transcript row and syllabus outcomes. | A failed grade, credit shortfall or missing evidence has a visible reason. Model similarity cannot override it. |
| Content | Staff reads the two descriptions and records which mandatory outcomes are covered, including practical work. A renamed course may qualify; matching titles alone are insufficient. | Staff can choose a different candidate and must explain the selection. No automatic acceptance. |
| Award | One completed course can satisfy one destination requirement. Award destination credits once. Pilot cap: 50% of configured degree credits, counting prior recognised credits. Test exactly at cap and one credit above it. | Refuse double counting and above-cap awards. Missing degree total holds the decision. Record recognised credits separately from grade averages. |
| Records | Retain current duplicate check using previous ID + institution or similar name with matching phone/DOB. Restrict details to reviewers. | Staff records how the flag was resolved. An unresolved identity question holds the decision. |
| Authority | Student submits; departmental reviewer recommends; authorised academic admin decides with name, date and reason. One open admission-change request per student. | Only final approval changes academic status. Keep a dated decision and policy version for later inspection. |
| Cancellation | Student chooses admission, gives reason and acknowledges effect. Staff checks enrolment and any pending case. Admin approves or rejects; display receipt and decision times. | Approval cancels that admission. Rejection leaves it active or transferred as before. Financial clearance is a separate Accounts referral; no automatic payment. |

## Evidence and AI

Create two fictional students and one fictional sending institute in the Pune region. Use obviously synthetic record IDs. Produce a selectable-text PDF statement of marks for the transfer applicant with semester, code, title, grade scale, credits and result, plus a one-page source syllabus with short numbered learning outcomes. Watermark each page “Fictional academic record — university project”. Omit signatures and certification marks. Use the same fixture for database seeding, PDF generation and expected outcomes. Give the cancellation student a separate admission record and a dated acknowledgement. No personal or real student data is needed.

Prioritise the **staff course comparison**. Today the engine ranks normalised titles and consumes a candidate as soon as a title clears the review threshold. This makes results sensitive to course order. Show ranked alternatives per destination course, reserving a source only when staff accepts it. Keep the existing title and alias search as a baseline.

Use [all-MiniLM-L6-v2](https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2), an English sentence embedding model, through [Transformers.js local inference](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers.js/custom_usage). Pin the ONNX artifact revision and license, cache weights under the project, disable runtime remote loading and preload before the pitch. Encode short outcomes separately, precompute the destination catalog, and rank the closest evidence for each source/destination pair. Display the actual excerpts, missing outcomes and the eligibility decision separately. Keep name, DOB and contact data outside the model input. No similarity score is a probability of academic equivalence.

Feasibility gate: compare with the existing matcher on 12 labelled sample pairs, including paraphrases, misleading identical titles, an unrelated course, lab/theory mismatch and ambiguous evidence. Record top-3 retrieval, false suggestions, cold load and repeat timings on the presentation laptop. Aim for ≤2 seconds for a warm four-course comparison. Require a correct improvement on at least one paraphrase and zero awards that breach hard rules. This small fixture is a regression check; publish measured results and sample size. On missing weights or timeout show the title-based comparison and allow manual review. OCR on scans and generative explanations come later; a readable PDF plus confirmed fields already supports the core decision.

## Phase 1 verification

The seed includes a fictional IT transfer into semester 3 of B.Tech. CSE and a separate CSE cancellation applicant. The marks statement and syllabus derive from `backend/config/computingPilot.json`; rebuild the PDF using `scripts/build-pilot-evidence.py` with ReportLab. The configured degree total is 160 credits and the ceiling is 80 including prior recognised credits. This four-subject catalog covers the pilot review, not the full degree.

An isolated Node/CPU trial used Transformers.js 3.8.1 and quantized Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 revision 751bff37182d3f1213fa05d7196b954e230abad9, with remote model loading disabled. Four short paraphrases (tree traversal, SQL, matrix algebra and stack operations) each ranked the corresponding passage first among four options. One run loaded the local model in 288 ms and encoded and ranked four queries against cached destination vectors in 14 ms. These are preliminary laptop measurements. The staff screen currently uses the existing title/alias comparison. Integration and the larger evaluation belong to Phase 2.
